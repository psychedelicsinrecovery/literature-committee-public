# Staying Connected: Parking Lots, Zoom & Discord

> **DISCUSSION DRAFT** · Practical member guidance, open to feedback. Not a fellowship-wide policy.

## What is a PIR® parking lot?

A Zoom parking lot is the informal time before or after a scheduled meeting. Come and go as you please. Camera on or off, talk or just listen. There is no formal welcome or raised-hand sharing queue. Leave room for others and follow the [Safety and Ethics guidance](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/safety-and-ethics/).

### If something comes up

- **Need help in the room?** Privately message the Zoom host if someone has claimed host. For Zoom help outside the room, look for **Zoom Concierge** on the [WhatsApp support page](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/whatsapp/).
- **Conversation getting crowded?** Pause and leave space for others. Don't record, screenshot, or repeat another person's share without permission. If someone keeps disrupting the space or breaks the safety rules, the host may intervene, remove them, or restrict access where possible.
- **Host needs to go?** If you claimed host, pass it to a willing person before leaving so the room isn't reset. The room usually winds down when the last person leaves. Wrap up sooner if another scheduled meeting needs the Zoom link.

## New to Zoom?

### Join your first meeting

1. Open the meeting link from the [online meetings list](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/online-meetings/). If Zoom asks, open its app or choose **Join from your browser** when that option is offered.
2. Enter the name you want others to see. If there's a waiting room, wait for the host to admit you.
3. Choose **Join Audio** and pick your device audio. Start muted; the camera is optional unless the meeting asks otherwise.
4. Follow the chair's instructions for sharing. When asked to raise your hand, look under **Reactions**. If you dialed in by phone, use star-9 (\*9) to raise your hand and star-6 (\*6) to mute or unmute.

### If you can't hear or be heard

Check that you've joined audio, that the right speaker and microphone are selected, and that you're not muted. On a phone, check that Zoom has microphone permission. You can privately message a host if you need help and chat is available.

For more help, visit [Zoom Concierge](https://chat.whatsapp.com/HU1J7u39MhjLmqbY6Zp2kE?mode=gi_t) or the [PIR® WhatsApp support page](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/whatsapp/).

## New to Discord?

### Find your way around

1. Open the [PIR® Discord invitation](https://discord.gg/MyprTq8w95) in a browser or in the Discord app. Sign in or make an account if you need one.
2. Read the welcome information and rules before posting. Choose a display name you're comfortable sharing; don't post another member's personal details or recovery story.
3. Text channels are for messages. Voice channels are optional drop-in spaces; select one to join, check your microphone settings, and disconnect when you're done.

### What to expect

The space is available for informal connection. It's not currently staffed, monitored in real time, or hosting scheduled programming. You can look around without introducing yourself.
