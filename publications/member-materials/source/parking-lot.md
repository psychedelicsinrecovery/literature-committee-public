# What is a PIR® parking lot?

> **DISCUSSION DRAFT** · Practical member guidance, open to feedback. Not a fellowship-wide policy.

## Drop in before or after a meeting

A Zoom parking lot is the informal time before or after a scheduled meeting. Come and go as you please. Camera on or off, talk or just listen. There is no formal welcome or raised-hand sharing queue. Leave room for others and follow the [Safety and Ethics guidance](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/safety-and-ethics/).

## If something comes up

- **Need help in the room?** Privately message the Zoom host if someone has claimed host. For Zoom help outside the room, look for **Zoom Concierge** on the [WhatsApp support page](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/whatsapp/).
- **Conversation getting crowded?** Pause and leave space for others. Don't record, screenshot, or repeat another person's share without permission. If someone keeps disrupting the space or breaks the safety rules, the host may intervene, remove them, or restrict access where possible.
- **Host needs to go?** If you claimed host, pass it to a willing person before leaving so the room isn't reset. The room usually winds down when the last person leaves. Wrap up sooner if another scheduled meeting needs the Zoom link.
